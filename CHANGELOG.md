# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Menu-bar popover: noise control (Noise Cancelling, Ambient with level and Focus on voice, Off),
  listening mode, Background Music room size, equalizer presets and the Custom curve,
  speak-to-chat, the multipoint playback source, and the battery level and charging state.
- Desktop widget in small and medium sizes, with buttons for the three noise-control modes and,
  at the medium size, the equalizer presets.
- A background agent that holds the headphones' single control session only while it is needed,
  so Sony's phone app can still connect.
- `xm6ctl`, a command-line client for the agent.
